# SMS suppliers and pricing assessment

## What is implemented now

BeanSMS is a registered, disabled candidate supplier. Its official reseller API is documented at https://www.beansms.com/developers (key plus secret, REST status polling, catalogue and balance reads). Its terms describe an intermediary dependent on upstream providers: https://www.beansms.com/terms. This is not independent verification of reliability, wholesale pricing, stock independence or profit. Do not preload a large supplier wallet until a small controlled trial succeeds.

Run `supabase/sms-providers-upgrade.sql` after platform upgrades. `/admin/sms-providers` has private supplier records, exact service/country mappings, the latest 500 supplier order records and an authenticated, read-only BeanSMS catalogue check. Put `BEANSMS_API_KEY` and `BEANSMS_API_SECRET` only in server/Vercel environment variables. No secrets are stored in supplier tables. Customers do not receive this private supplier ledger. Existing 5SIM web/mobile/bot orders are backfilled/tagged without changing their IDs; new web/mobile/bot purchases record raw quoted/actual supplier amounts when available. Historical acquisition costs remain unknown.

The web SMS selection now fetches a live quote instead of showing mock-data price estimates; purchase re-quotes server-side and requires reconfirmation if its price exceeds the customer's displayed maximum. The country selector only lists the 13 codes supported by the current purchase API, not unavailable static entries. Prices are not frozen indefinitely. No existing markup was changed.

## Activation still required

BeanSMS customer purchases are **not wired or enabled**. The authenticated response shapes, price currency, country identifiers and stock must first be inspected. No cross-provider country numbers should be guessed (the documentation's country 12 example is not a general mapping table). Confirm API/reseller terms, supplier funding fees, billing currency and actual wallet debit on staging.

Then implement/verify the complete provider adapter behind the same canonical service/country catalogue: quotes, allocation, persistent order provider association, completion, status, timeout, cancellation, supplier refund confirmation, bot notifications, cron and customer refund review. Every existing 5SIM-specific status/refund consumer must dispatch using the saved supplier association, never a customer-supplied provider value. The present tracking trigger intentionally labels existing flow as 5SIM; it must be replaced with explicit transactional provider assignment before any BeanSMS order is persisted. The old unique supplier order ID in `sms_orders` must also become a namespaced/public local ID before a second supplier can coexist without collisions. Provider failures must never create a second activation automatically after an uncertain purchase response.

`eligibleSmsOffers` is a tested routing primitive, not yet a customer checkout router. It merges only explicitly mapped, enabled, fresh, positive-stock offers and orders them by cost. Use reliability/delivery success and refund settlement as additional qualification gates before choosing the cheapest offer. The published BeanSMS webhook material inspected here did not document a signature-verification contract; do not expose a trusted webhook until authentication is established. Polling is the initial safe path.

An uncertain purchase needs a durable attempt record, wallet reservation, reconciliation with supplier order history and a compensation path before retry/failover. The existing allocation/wallet-debit atomicity launch blocker remains. These changes do not claim to solve it.

## Current revenue model

Default settings are a 3× supplier-cost markup, followed by the requested 2% retail increase and rounding. Ignoring rounding, a 100 XAF supplier cost becomes 306 XAF customer price: 206 XAF gross spread, about 67.3% gross margin. This is a conditional calculation, not observed profit. Database settings and SMM overrides may differ from the default. The current SMS implementation assumes supplier quote units are USD and uses 600 XAF/USD; that assumption must be verified against the supplier's actual wallet debit and currency before relying on price/profit reports. The 5SIM API documentation does not explicitly establish quote currency in the sections inspected: https://5sim.net/docs. Consequently new accounting records keep currency/cost verification unset until reconciliation.

Net contribution = customer service revenue minus actual settled supplier cost, funding/FX costs, payment processing costs, customer refunds net of supplier recoveries, and variable support/fraud costs. Wallet deposits are liabilities, not service profit. Supplier-wallet funding is not the same as the cost of completed activations. Avoid counting both supplier funding and consumed activation costs as expenses twice. Unused-deposit returns are not service refunds; reservation-release ledger credits are not new sales or supplier refunds.

Recommendation: keep prices unchanged until we compare the same service + country + quality across suppliers. For commodity services, test a lower base multiplier around 1.8–2.2 (effective 1.836–2.244 after the 2% increase) on selected offerings if actual acquisition and operating costs permit it. That corresponds to roughly 45.5–55.4% gross margin before overhead. Hard-to-source countries or stronger service/support can justify a higher margin. This is a suggested experiment, not a guarantee or an automatic setting change. Measure completed-order conversion, code delivery rate, refunds, support time and net contribution rather than chasing a headline cheapest price.

BeanSMS authenticated reseller prices are not available in this workspace yet, so no defensible claim that it is cheaper than 5SIM can be made. Compare a small basket (e.g. WhatsApp/Google/Telegram across USA/UK/Nigeria/Cameroon), not supplier-wide starting prices from different countries.
